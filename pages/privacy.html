Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Information We Collect

ExcelOps (operated at GetExcelOps.com) collects the following information:

  • Email address — when you voluntarily subscribe to our newsletter or download a template.
  • Contact form data — name, email, and message when you submit our contact form.
  • Usage data — pages visited, time on site, referral source, and browser type via Google Analytics (anonymized).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To send you free Excel templates and tutorials you requested.
  • To respond to your contact form submissions.
  • To improve our website content based on usage patterns.
  • We do not sell your data to third parties.

3. Cookies

We use Google Analytics cookies to understand how visitors use our site. These are anonymized and do not identify individual users. You can opt out via your browser settings or the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following services which have their own privacy policies:

  • Mailchimp — email newsletter delivery
  • Formspree — contact form processing
  • Google Analytics — website analytics
  • Google AdSense — advertising (when enabled)

5. Data Retention

Email subscribers can unsubscribe at any time via the link in any email. Contact form submissions are retained for 12 months. Analytics data is retained per Google Analytics defaults (26 months).

6. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at the address on our Contact page. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions, use our Contact page.
