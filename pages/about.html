Our Mission

ExcelOps exists because most Excel resources teach you how to use features. We teach you how to solve real business problems. There is a big difference.

A generic Excel tutorial teaches you what a VLOOKUP is. We teach you how to use VLOOKUP to look up the reorder point for every SKU in your inventory list — and then highlight the ones that need ordering today.
