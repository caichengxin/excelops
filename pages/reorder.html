📐 The Formula

Reorder Point = (Average Daily Sales × Lead Time) + Safety Stock

If your stock is at or below this number, place an order now to avoid a stockout before the next delivery arrives.

📊 Avg Daily Sales

Average units sold per day over the last 30–90 days. Pull from your sales report.

🚚 Lead Time

Days from placing an order to receiving stock. Include supplier processing + shipping time.

🛡️ Safety Stock

Buffer for demand spikes or delays. Rule of thumb: (Max Daily Sales − Avg Daily Sales) × Lead Time.
