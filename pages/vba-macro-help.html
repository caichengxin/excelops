FAQ

Questions about VBA Macro Help

Can you fix an existing macro?

Yes. Share the workbook structure, the error message, and what the macro should do. The fix can be targeted instead of rewriting everything.

Can a macro be replaced with Power Query?

Sometimes. If the task is mostly data import, cleanup, and transformation, Power Query may be easier to maintain than VBA.

Can you explain old VBA code?

Yes. A plain-English explanation can help your team understand the workflow before deciding whether to fix, replace, or retire it.
